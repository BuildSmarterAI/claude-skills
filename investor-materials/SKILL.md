# Investor Materials

Build investor-facing materials that are consistent, credible, and defensible in institutional meetings.

## When to Activate

- Creating or revising a pitch deck
- Writing an investor memo or one-pager
- Building a SaaS financial model or pro forma
- Preparing for sovereign wealth fund conversations
- Aligning multiple fundraising docs around one source of truth
- Updating traction metrics across existing materials

## Golden Rule

All investor materials must agree with each other. Create or confirm a single source of truth before writing:

- Portfolio entities and their relationships
- Traction metrics (MRR, ARR, users, reports generated, projects completed)
- Pricing and revenue assumptions per product
- Raise size, instrument, and valuation expectations
- Use of funds allocation
- Team bios and titles (Harris = Founder & Operator across all entities)
- Milestones and timelines

If conflicting numbers appear, stop and resolve before drafting.

## Asset Guidance

### Pitch Deck (Institutional / SWF)

Recommended flow:
1. Company + wedge (the ecosystem advantage)
2. Problem (manual feasibility, inaccurate estimates, fragmented data)
3. Solution (integrated intelligence platform)
4. Product demo / screenshots
5. Market (TAM: $XB PropTech + ConTech, SAM: Texas CRE)
6. Business model (SaaS tiers + per-project pricing)
7. Traction (MRR, reports generated, projects completed, pipeline)
8. Data moat (340+ projects, 2.65M parcels, 11 counties, proprietary indices)
9. Team (operator-built, 15+ years construction)
10. Competition / differentiation (kill factor detection, 60-second delivery)
11. Ask (raise size, use of funds, milestones)
12. Appendix (detailed financials, architecture diagram, customer list)

**Institutional formatting standards:**
- Currency: $1,234,567
- Percentages: 1 decimal (12.5%)
- Cap rates: X.XX%
- Multiples: 0.0x
- Right-align all numbers
- Source every data point
- No "revolutionary" or "game-changing" language

### One-Pager

Structure:
1. One sentence: what BuildSmarter Holdings does
2. The flywheel diagram (visual)
3. Key metrics (3-5 proof points)
4. Market opportunity (1-2 sentences)
5. The ask (precise amount, instrument, use)
6. Contact info

### Financial Model (SaaS)

Include:
- Revenue by product (SiteIntel, ConstructIntel, Maxx Builders)
- SaaS metrics: MRR, ARR, churn, LTV, CAC, NRR
- Pricing tiers and assumptions per product
- Customer acquisition by channel (SEO, cold email, referral, paid)
- Bear / base / bull scenarios
- Milestone-linked spending
- Monthly burn rate and runway
- Break-even analysis

### Investor Memo

Structure:
1. Executive summary (what, why now, what we need)
2. Problem depth (with quantified pain points)
3. Solution and product
4. Market and competition
5. Business model and unit economics
6. Traction and proof points
7. Team and unfair advantages
8. Use of funds and milestones
9. Risks and mitigations

## SaaS Metrics Definitions

Use these consistently across all materials:

| Metric | Definition |
|--------|-----------|
| MRR | Monthly recurring revenue (sum of all active subscriptions) |
| ARR | MRR × 12 |
| Churn | % of customers lost per month |
| NRR | Net revenue retention (expansion - churn) |
| LTV | Average revenue per customer × average lifespan |
| CAC | Total sales + marketing spend / new customers acquired |
| LTV:CAC | Target >3:1 |

## Red Flags to Avoid

- Unverifiable claims or inflated metrics
- TAM/SAM without bottom-up sanity check
- Revenue math that doesn't sum cleanly
- Inconsistent metrics across deck, model, and memo
- "We have no competition" (always acknowledge alternatives)
- Inflated certainty where assumptions are fragile

## Quality Gate

Before delivering:

- [ ] Every number matches the current source of truth
- [ ] Use of funds and revenue projections sum correctly
- [ ] Assumptions are visible, not buried
- [ ] SaaS metrics calculated consistently
- [ ] Formatting meets institutional standards (right-aligned numbers, sourced data)
- [ ] Story is clear without hype language
- [ ] Material is defensible in a partner meeting
- [ ] Portfolio flywheel narrative is clear
