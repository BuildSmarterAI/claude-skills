# Investor Outreach

Write investor communication that is short, personalized, and easy to act on.

## When to Activate

- Writing a cold email to an investor
- Drafting a warm intro request
- Sending follow-ups after a meeting or no response
- Writing investor updates during a fundraising process
- Tailoring outreach based on fund thesis or partner fit
- Preparing for sovereign wealth fund conversations

## Core Rules

1. Personalize every outbound message — reference their thesis, portfolio, or public statements.
2. Keep the ask low-friction — "15 minutes" not "a partnership."
3. Use proof, not adjectives — "340+ completed projects, $127M volume" not "impressive track record."
4. Stay concise — investors scan, they don't read.
5. Never send generic copy that could go to any investor.
6. No "revolutionary", "game-changing", or "disrupting" — operators don't talk like that.

## Investor Targeting Segments

### Sovereign Wealth Funds (SWF)
- Lead with scale: Texas CRE market size, data moat, institutional-grade platform
- Emphasize regulatory compliance, data governance, audit trails
- Formal tone, longer-form acceptable
- Reference their existing real estate or technology allocations

### PropTech / ConTech VCs
- Lead with the flywheel: construction data → cost intelligence → feasibility → PM
- Reference their portfolio companies (Procore, PlanGrid, Briq, etc.)
- Show how BuildSmarter fills gaps in their portfolio
- Metric-forward: MRR, growth rate, unit economics

### Strategic Investors (GCs, Developers, CRE Firms)
- Lead with operational value — they'd use the product AND invest
- Reference their project types and how SiteIntel/ConstructIntel applies
- Shorter pitch, more "here's what this does for your deals"

### Family Offices / Angels
- Lead with operator credibility — 15+ years, 340+ projects, built the GC first
- More personal tone, founder story matters
- Clear path to returns

## Cold Email Structure

```
Subject: [Specific, short — reference their thesis or a mutual connection]

[One sentence: why this investor specifically]

[Two sentences: what BuildSmarter does and the proof point]

[One sentence: the specific ask]

[Sign-off: name, title, one credibility anchor]
```

### Example (PropTech VC)

```
Subject: Construction cost intelligence — 340 projects of proprietary data

[Name] — I saw your investment in [portfolio company] and your thesis on
vertical AI in construction.

We're building ConstructIntel.ai, an AI cost intelligence platform powered
by proprietary data from 340+ completed Texas commercial projects ($127M+
volume). Our sister company Maxx Builders generates the project data that
makes our AI accurate — a flywheel no competitor can replicate.

Would 15 minutes make sense to walk through the platform and our metrics?

Harris Khan
Founder, BuildSmarter Holdings
```

## Personalization Sources

Reference one or more of:
- Relevant portfolio companies (especially construction/PropTech/CRE)
- Public thesis, talk, post, or article about PropTech/ConTech
- Mutual connection (strongest signal)
- Their real estate or infrastructure allocation strategy (for SWFs)
- A deal they did in Texas or in your vertical

If context is missing, ask for it or mark the draft as needing personalization.

## Follow-Up Cadence

```
Day 0:  Initial outbound
Day 4:  Short follow-up with one new data point (new customer, metric, feature)
Day 10: Final follow-up with clean close ("If timing isn't right, happy to
        reconnect next quarter")
```

Don't keep nudging past the third touch unless they've shown interest.

## Warm Intro Requests

Make life easy for the connector:

```
Hey [Connector] — would you be open to introducing me to [Investor] at [Fund]?

They invest in [relevant thesis] and we're building AI-powered construction
intelligence backed by proprietary data from 340+ commercial projects.

Here's a forwardable blurb:

---
Harris Khan is the founder of BuildSmarter Holdings, an integrated
construction intelligence ecosystem in Texas. The flagship products —
SiteIntel (GIS feasibility) and ConstructIntel.ai (AI cost intelligence) —
are powered by proprietary project data from Maxx Builders, his commercial
GC with 340+ completed projects. He's raising [amount] to scale the
platform across Texas.
---

Happy to send a one-pager if helpful. Thanks for considering it.
```

## Post-Meeting Follow-Up

```
Subject: Follow-up: [specific topic discussed]

[Name] — thanks for the time today.

[Answer to the specific question they asked or data point they requested]

[One new proof point if available]

[Clear next step: "Happy to send the financial model" or "Would a follow-up
with our technical lead be useful?"]
```

## Investor Update Emails (During Process)

Monthly or bi-weekly during active fundraise:

```
Subject: BuildSmarter Update — [Month] [Year]

Key metrics:
- MRR: $XX,XXX (↑ X% MoM)
- New customers: X
- Pipeline: $XXX,XXX in qualified opportunities

Product update:
- [One concrete milestone]

Fundraise update:
- [Status: conversations, terms, commitments]

Next milestone:
- [What you're focused on this month]
```

## Quality Gate

Before delivering:

- [ ] Message is personalized to the specific investor
- [ ] Ask is explicit and low-friction
- [ ] No fluff, no begging, no hype language
- [ ] Proof points are concrete and verifiable
- [ ] Metrics match current source of truth
- [ ] Word count stays tight (cold emails under 150 words)
- [ ] Tone matches the investor segment (formal for SWF, direct for VC)
