# Cold Outreach Pipeline

Turn permit data and industry leads into qualified conversations for ConstructIntel.ai and SiteIntel.

## When to Activate

- Building cold email sequences for TDLR permit leads
- Writing outreach to GCs, developers, architects, or lenders
- Processing permit lead lists for segmentation
- Creating follow-up sequences
- Optimizing email deliverability and response rates

## Lead Sources

### TDLR Permit Data (Primary)
Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation permit filings — companies pulling permits are actively building and need cost intelligence and feasibility tools.

**Segmentation from permit data:**
- **Project value** — $3-15M = Maxx Builders sweet spot, ConstructIntel target
- **Building type** — healthcare, retail, industrial, hospitality, institutional
- **Location** — DFW, Houston, Austin, San Antonio (11 county coverage)
- **Company type** — GC (ConstructIntel), developer (SiteIntel), architect (both)
- **Permit recency** — last 30 days = highest intent

### Other Lead Sources
- Construction industry directories (Blue Book, ABC, AGC membership)
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator (GC owners, VPs of preconstruction, estimators)
- ConstructConnect/Dodge project leads
- Local business journal project announcements
- Commercial permit databases by municipality

## Personalization Rules

Every cold email MUST include:
1. **Their company name** in the subject line
2. **Their specific project or activity** (permit, project announcement, portfolio)
3. **Their metro/market** (Houston, DFW, Austin, San Antonio)
4. **Their role-specific pain point** (estimator = accuracy, developer = speed, architect = site data)

**Never send:**
- Generic "Dear Sir/Madam" emails
- Emails without a specific project or activity reference
- The same email to GCs and developers (different pain points)

## Lead Scoring

| Signal | Score |
|--------|-------|
| Pulled permit in last 30 days | +30 |
| Project value $3-15M | +20 |
| In SiteIntel's 11 county coverage | +15 |
| Multiple permits in last 90 days (repeat builder) | +20 |
| Has estimator or preconstruction role on team | +10 |
| Responded to previous email | +25 |
| Visited website after email | +15 |

**Priority tiers:**
- 80+ = Call within 24 hours
- 50-79 = Full email sequence
- 30-49 = Add to nurture sequence
- <30 = Low priority, batch outreach

## Deliverability Rules

- Keep emails under 150 words
- No images in cold emails
- No more than 1 link per email
- Warm up new sending domains (50/day → 100/day → 200/day over 2 weeks)
- Use plain text, not HTML templates
- Personalize subject lines (never batch the same subject)
- Clean bounce-backs immediately

## Quality Gate

Before sending any sequence:

- [ ] Every email is personalized (company, project, metro, role)
- [ ] Subject line is specific, not generic
- [ ] Ask is low-friction (10 minutes, free report, quick look)
- [ ] No hype language — operator tone only
- [ ] Proof points are concrete (340+ projects, 60 seconds, 10+ sources)
- [ ] Sequence has 3 touches max (don't spam)
- [ ] Lead scoring applied to prioritize high-value targets
