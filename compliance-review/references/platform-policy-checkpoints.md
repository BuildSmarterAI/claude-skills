# Platform Policy Checkpoints

**Status: dated checklist, reviewed 2026-08-30. Not a policy snapshot.**

This file records **where to look and what to look for**. It deliberately does not restate any platform's rules, because those change on no notice and a stale rule stated confidently is worse than no rule at all.

> Before any launch, read the current policy at the source below. If you cannot, mark the item `NEEDS-HUMAN` in the review rather than relying on this file.

## How to use this

For each platform the asset will run on:

1. Open the current policy page.
2. Walk the "check for" list below and find the current rule for each.
3. Record in the review: the risk, the rule as it reads today, and the date you checked.
4. Anything you could not verify is `NEEDS-HUMAN`, never `CLEAR`.

## Cross-platform: what to check everywhere

These risk *categories* have been durable across platforms and years. The specific rules attached to them have not.

- **Personal-attribute assertions** - copy that implies knowledge of the viewer ("as someone struggling with debt") rather than describing the product.
- **Protected classes** - targeting or implied targeting on race, religion, health, sexual orientation, immigration status, age, disability, financial hardship.
- **Housing, employment, credit** - most platforms run a separate, stricter regime for these. Assume special-category handling applies and verify.
- **Unrealistic outcomes** - income claims, health outcomes, guaranteed results.
- **Before-and-after imagery** and body-related claims.
- **Countdown and pressure mechanics** - especially timers that reset.
- **Landing-page mismatch** - the destination must deliver what the ad promised. This is enforced by every major platform and is the most common quiet rejection.
- **Restricted and prohibited categories** - crypto, supplements, gambling, firearms, tobacco, adult, political and social issue advertising, pharmaceuticals.
- **Trademark and competitor naming** - rules differ sharply between search and social.
- **Disclosure requirements** - paid partnership, affiliate, endorsement, AI-generated content labelling.

## Where to look, per platform

| Platform | Policy home | Check for |
|---|---|---|
| Google Ads | Advertising Policies Help Centre | Misrepresentation, unreliable claims, restricted categories, trademark rules, destination-experience requirements, personalised-advertising restrictions |
| Microsoft Advertising | Advertising Policies | Largely parallel to Google, with its own disallowed-content and editorial rules |
| Meta (Facebook / Instagram) | Advertising Standards | Personal attributes, special ad categories, unrealistic outcomes, before/after, restricted content, text and landing-page rules |
| LinkedIn | Advertising Policies | Professional-context rules, targeting restrictions, employment-related special handling |
| TikTok | Advertising Policies | Prohibited industries, claim substantiation, creative and disclosure rules, minors |
| X | Ads Policies | Restricted categories, political content rules |
| Pinterest | Advertising Guidelines | Health, weight-loss, and claim restrictions |
| Reddit | Advertising Policy | Community and content restrictions |
| Amazon Ads | Advertising Guidelines | Claim substantiation, comparative claims |
| Email (CAN-SPAM, CASL, GDPR/ePrivacy) | Regulator guidance for each jurisdiction | Consent basis, sender identification, physical address, unsubscribe mechanics and honouring window |
| SMS (TCPA and local equivalents) | Regulator and carrier guidance | Express written consent, opt-out handling, quiet hours |

## Regulatory regimes to name in a review

Name the regime so a human knows which specialist to route to. Do not attempt to apply it.

- **Advertising standards** - the national advertising regulator or self-regulatory body for each target market.
- **Consumer protection** - deceptive-practice rules, endorsement and testimonial guidance, "dark pattern" enforcement.
- **Sector regimes** - financial promotions, health and medical device claims, supplement claims, legal and immigration services advertising, education outcome claims.
- **Fair housing, fair lending, and equal employment** - where housing, credit, or employment is advertised.
- **Privacy and consent** - where the copy references tracking, personalisation, or data use.

## What this file must never become

A list of specific thresholds, character limits, or prohibited phrases copied from a platform. Those go stale within months, and the failure mode is silent: a review reads `CLEAR` against a rule that no longer exists. Keep this file structural.
